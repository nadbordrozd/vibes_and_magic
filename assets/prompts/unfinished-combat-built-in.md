# Unfinished combat roster — built-in source prompts

All six sources were generated with the built-in image-generation tool, one request per unit. The
large keyed sources are under `assets/sources/unfinished-roster/`; the installed chroma-key helper
produced the transparent intermediates under `.pixel-work/unfinished-roster-keyed/`, and
`scripts/buildUnfinishedRoster.py` performs the deterministic native-canvas reduction.

```text
Primary request: create Candle-Wisps, the tier-one unit of The Unfinished faction: a small clustered troop of three floating candle flames sheltered inside separate little hollow folds of ash-white funeral linen, reaching forward together as if trying to remember a task.
Subject: three clearly separate small wisps forming one compact readable unit silhouette; each has a simple candle-gold flame and a dark empty opening beneath it; no faces, no weapons.
Style/medium: richly drawn warm 1990s storybook fantasy strategy-game character art, crisp dark selective outlines, compact painterly color clusters, readable silhouette, not modern concept art, not grimdark.
Composition/framing: strict side view facing right, complete unit fully visible with generous padding, lowest linen tips share a clear horizontal baseline, compact rather than tall.
Lighting/mood: soft upper-left light; gentle, mournful, precise; the dead are unresolved rather than evil.
Color palette: bone white, ash gray, old ivory, muted candle gold, tiny warm orange flames; absolutely no green-black necromancer palette.
Scene/backdrop: perfectly flat solid #ff00ff chroma-key background for removal, one uniform color, no floor plane.
Constraints: one static combat unit; strong silhouette at small size; no cast shadow, no contact shadow, no scenery, no ground, no aura, no glow bloom, no smoke, no gore, no text, no logo, no watermark; do not use #ff00ff anywhere in the subject.
```
