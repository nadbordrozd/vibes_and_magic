## Granite scatter (six subjects)

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: source sheet for six small adventure-map mountain sprites
Input images: Image 1 is a style and palette reference only; do not copy its existing mountains.
Primary request: create six genuinely different low granite outcrops and boulder groups for the transition edge of a mountain range.
Scene/backdrop: perfectly flat solid #ff00ff chroma-key background, one uniform color with no shadows, gradients, texture, floor plane, or lighting variation.
Subject: six separate self-contained rocky pieces arranged in a clean 3 by 2 grid with very generous empty spacing; include a split slab, offset boulder trio, broken shelf, low jagged teeth, cracked pair, and one flat scree fan.
Style/medium: warm cute 1990s storybook strategy-game pixel art matching Image 1; enlarged low-resolution pixel-art preview with coarse deliberate square pixel clusters, crisp hard edges, no antialiasing.
Composition/framing: oblique front-on view with a slight overhead tilt; every piece is wider than tall, asymmetric, and descends to pebble height at both left and right ends; all six have comparable apparent scale suitable for a 32 by 48 native canvas.
Lighting/mood: light from the lower right with internal rock shading toward the upper left; no cast shadow.
Color palette: charcoal and warm grey granite with restrained moss-green accents on rock faces only.
Constraints: six isolated pieces only; bare rock contacts; generous separation; do not use #ff00ff in the subjects.
Avoid: one large mountain divided into pieces, tall peaks, symmetric pyramids, grass skirts, dirt plates, flowers, trees, landscape, horizon, caves, buildings, text, UI, watermark.
```
