## Granite top-down ridges

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: source sheet for four reusable adventure-map mountain ridge landforms
Input images: Image 1 is the Heroes of Might and Magic II composition and camera reference; study how its mountain groups occupy broad ground areas and overlap, but do not reproduce its UI, map layout, buildings, or exact mountains. Image 2 establishes the approved warm granite palette and hand-placed pixel treatment. Image 3 shows the current failure: repeated frontal triangular façades and visible lanes between rows; correct that failure rather than copying it.
Primary request: create four genuinely different broad granite ridge landforms designed to stack in overlapping rows into one dense natural mountain region.
Scene/backdrop: perfectly flat solid #ff00ff chroma-key background, one uniform color with no shadows, gradients, texture, floor plane, or lighting variation.
Subject: four separate self-contained ridges arranged in a clean 2 by 2 grid with very generous empty spacing; designs are a broad folded shoulder with two rear crowns, a shallow horseshoe ridge with a low foreground spur, a long diagonal hogback with offset rock shelves, and an irregular three-lobed upland with a rear saddle.
Style/medium: cute warm 1990s storybook strategy-game pixel art matching Image 2; enlarged low-resolution pixel-art preview with coarse deliberate square pixel clusters, crisp hard edges, no antialiasing.
Composition/framing: noticeably higher and more top-down than Image 3, while remaining oblique and non-isometric; broad visible upper rock planes and long descending foreground slopes; each landform fills a wide roughly 3-by-2-tile visual footprint, is much wider than tall, and has an irregular oval or trapezoidal ground silhouette rather than a thin horizontal baseline. The left and right extremes descend to pebble height.
Lighting/mood: light from the lower right, bright right-facing planes, dark left faces, internal shadows toward the upper left; no cast shadow.
Color palette: charcoal and warm grey granite, cream highlights, dark creases, restrained moss-green accents on rock faces only, matching Image 2.
Constraints: four isolated connected landforms only; dense opaque rocky footprints suitable for covering the row behind; generous separation; do not use #ff00ff in the subjects.
Avoid: frontal triangular mountain icons, repeated centered peaks, thin sawtooth strips, vertical cliff walls, grass skirts, dirt plates, square or diamond bases, flowers, trees, snow, caves, buildings, landscape, horizon, text, UI, border, watermark.
```
