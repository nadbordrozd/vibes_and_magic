# Artifact System & Catalog

## System

- **11 equipment slots** per hero: Head, Cloak, Amulet, Weapon, Shield, Armor, Ring ×2, Boots, Misc ×2. Paper-doll UI on the hero screen; unequipped artifacts sit in a backpack (unlimited, adventure-only).
- Equip/unequip freely on the adventure map; **never during combat** (fluidity rule).
- Sources: guardian drops, puzzle-locks, special map sites (19), rarely chests. Never sold in shops (Peddler R2 may *sell* at 60%). **Defeated heroes drop all carried artifacts** to the victor — including ultimate pieces (established rule).
- **Trinket migration:** the six trinkets from 17 become Misc-slot artifacts; they no longer occupy consumable inventory. Their effects are unchanged.
- Classes: **Vanilla** (stat/simple number), **Charm** (small behavior), **Relic** (build-around), **the Ultimate Set** (unique, 4 pieces). Rarity for drop tables: Vanilla=C, Charm=U, Relic=R.

1. **Falconer's Glove** (Misc): Scouting-style exact counts at 2 tiles (no skill needed).
2. **Whetstone of the Clans** (Misc): your `beast` stacks +1 speed.
3. **Tinker's Spectacles** (Head): see every guardian's drop before fighting.
4. **The Quiet Horseshoe** (Misc): neutral guardians ≤25% your power flee instead of fighting (free tile).
5. **Standard-Bearer's Baldric** (Cloak): +3 meter/round to your stacks (stacks with Command).
6. **Salt-Crusted Compass** (Amulet): see all Trade Goods values map-wide; your Trade Goods +25%.

8. **Forge-Ash Gauntlets** (Weapon-hand pair, Misc): your Burn applications +1.
9. **The Bee-Charmer's Veil** (Head): resin/thicket/undergrowth cost your stacks nothing.
10. **Purse of the Prudent Toad** (Misc): +200 gold/day.
11. **Chalkmaster's Ring** (Ring): your Wall of the Maker creates 1 extra hex.
12. **The Second Quiver** (Misc): your `ranged` stacks +6 shots.

## Relics (12) — the crazy shelf

1. **The Sundered Hourglass** (Amulet, Unstruck Bell provenance): your hero may cast twice per round on even-numbered rounds.
2. **The Longest Candle** (Misc, Unfinished): the first allied stack destroyed each battle returns at 25% size at round end.
3. **The Crooked Distaff** (Weapon, Hagwood): enemy luck −2 in your battles.
4. **Banner of the First Field** (Cloak): your stacks' meters trigger at 80 instead of 100.
5. **The Patchwork Standard** (Cloak): no mixed-faction meter penalty; each distinct faction in your army grants all stacks +5 meter/round. *The cross-faction enabler.*
6. **Seamstone** (Amulet): your battles are resonant in a school chosen when you equip it (overrides tile resonance).
7. **Mirrorshard Pendant** (Amulet, Gloaming): the first spell cast against your army each battle is Reflected back at its caster.
8. **The Bell's Clapper** (Weapon, stolen from the monastery — equipping it is a lore event): once per battle, set ALL meters on the field, both sides, to 0.
9. **Queen's Amber** (Ring, Vespiary): your T1 dwellings +50% growth.
10. **Wolf-Mother's Torc** (Amulet, Wildergrass): your `beast` stacks retaliate twice per round.
11. **Horn of the Broad World** (Misc): once per battle, your largest stack counts double size for one attack's damage.
12. **The Toy Knight's Heart** (Misc, Wound-Wright): one `construct` stack of your choice gains `unfinished_vow` (returns once at 50%).

## The Ultimate Set — The Tailor's Kit

Author-side: the instrument of the stitching itself. Player-side: an old sewing kit, absurdly mundane, and every faction's myths go quiet about it. Four pieces, **visible on the map from turn 1** (established rule), each guarded by an authored lock-grade guardian. Lootable from dead heroes like everything else.

- **The Needle** (Weapon) · **The Golden Thread** (Amulet) · **The Thimble** (Ring) · **The Patternbook** (Misc)
- Each piece alone: +2 to one primary stat (A / SP / D / K respectively) — deliberately modest.
- **2 pieces:** +2 all stats; all essence deposits and seams visible map-wide.
- **3 pieces:** all your spells resolve as their + faces.
- **4 pieces:** the above, plus your battles are resonant in all four schools, plus once per week — **Unstitch**: move this hero and army to any explored tile.

No victory condition attached; the Kit makes you monstrous, it doesn't end the game. Flavor text on assembly, one line: *"It has been used exactly once."*
