# Magic System — Four Schools, Six Pairs

## Structure

Four schools. Each playable faction's identity is a unique **pair** of schools — four schools yield exactly six pairs, one per faction. Two factions are **opposites** when their pairs share no school.

| School | Domain | Flavor |
|---|---|---|
| **Rite** | oaths, blessings, banners, sanctity, morale-meter effects | ceremony, the properly-done thing |
| **Craft** | making, binding, wards, mirrors, walls, transmutation | the made thing, the kept promise of matter |
| **Wild** | growth, beasts, weather, terrain change, forced movement | the growing thing, the moving sky |
| **Grave** | endings, memory, curses, fear, death triggers | the ended thing, remembered |

Faction pairs: Hearthguard = Rite+Craft · Unfinished = Rite+Grave · Wildergrass = Rite+Wild · Wound-Wrights = Craft+Grave · Vespiary = Craft+Wild · Hagwood = Wild+Grave.

## Rules

1. **Schools are player-facing magic; faction verbs are innate.** A faction's unit abilities are NOT school spells. Schools are what *heroes cast*. Keep the axes separate — this is what makes cross-faction acquisition combinatorial.
2. **Guilds deal from your pair.** Mage guild spell offers are weighted: ~80% your two schools, ~20% the two adjacent schools (one shared school with yours), 0% never applies — the remaining school appears only at trace rates via high guild levels. A faction can never reliably obtain its opposite's exclusive combinations at home. Off-pair magic comes from: captured towns, shrines, scroll/consumable pickups, and rare draft offers. This is deliberate: the spells you can't have at home are the ones that complete combos your faction can't build alone.
3. **No casting prerequisites.** Any hero can cast any learned spell. No school-skill permission gates. School investment, if any, is draftable potency perks ("Grave effects last +1 round"), never permission.
4. **Budget:** ~10 spells per school (~40 total). Per school: ~6 staples, 2 build-arounds (strong under a constructed precondition), 1 map-topology spell, 1 effect-targeting-effect spell.
5. The effect-targeting-effect family is distributed one per school: **Amplify** (Rite — double an active effect's magnitude), **Reflect** (Craft — mirror an effect onto another target), **Sour** (Grave — invert a beneficial effect), **Overgrow** (Wild — an active effect spreads to adjacent stacks).
6. Topology spells, one per school (details later): Rite = sanctuary-recall to a friendly town; Craft = gate/passage; Wild = travel by river/greenway; Grave = step between places where things died.
7. All spell effects hook named pipeline stages and obey the target-scaling law (03_MECHANICS): no flat-magnitude effect may be the answer to a hard fight.
8. Mana: pool 10×Knowledge, +1/day in field, full in friendly town (unchanged). Spell costs are the balance lever for build-arounds — big swings cost most of a pool.
